# Roadmap

What is being worked on, in order. Only decided work is listed. Released
changes are in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Now: version 1.0

Version 1.0 is in development. Built so far:

- Split view: two pages side by side.
- Today: todos and a calendar, next to the tabs.
- Journeys: a notebook for courses, books, words and a diary. It also checks
  chosen pages for changes.
- Zen: only the page on screen.
- A new design, in light and dark themes.
- A tour and short lessons.
- Third-party cookies blocked by default; site icons loaded without cookies.
- Site permissions and ad blocking follow the page that has loaded, not an
  address typed into the bar.

Plans can change before release. 1.0 has no fixed date; it comes out when its
tests pass. It will be free to use, and its licence terms will be published
with it. Versions up to 0.3.3 stay GPL-3.0.
